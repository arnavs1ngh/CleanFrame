# Photograph credits

Fifteen of the sixteen launcher photographs and their derived material images use the Unsplash License. See [the source and credit register](../../docs/asset-sources.md) for each photographer and photo page, and [SOURCES.json](wallpapers/SOURCES.json) for runtime paths.

The sixteenth, `family`, is a personal photo supplied directly by this installation's owner, not from Unsplash. It and its derived material images are not covered by the Unsplash License; keep it out of any public/export build (see `docs/public-release.md`).

These images are launcher backgrounds. Samsung Art Mode uses its own artwork and controls.
